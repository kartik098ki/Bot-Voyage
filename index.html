Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Bot-Voyage

How does Bot-Voyage work?
Bot-Voyage uses artificial intelligence to create personalized travel plans based on your preferences. Simply tell us where you want to go and what you're interested in, and our AI will generate a complete itinerary for you.
Is Bot-Voyage free to use?
Yes, Bot-Voyage is completely free to use for basic travel planning. We also offer premium features for a small fee, such as real-time updates and exclusive deals.
Can I collaborate with friends on a trip?
Absolutely! Bot-Voyage allows you to create a shared trip with friends and family. You can collaborate on the itinerary, split expenses, and make decisions together.
How accurate are the travel recommendations?
Our AI analyzes thousands of data points including user reviews, current events, weather conditions, and crowd levels to provide the most accurate and up-to-date recommendations possible.
